Sustainability

Our purpose

Building a sustainable future

Sustainability is fundamental to creating value for the business. We seek to innovate, create genuine impact and support long-term value creation — and our growth plans are underpinned by long-term leadership in sustainability.

We focus our efforts on three key areas: nature, places and people.

Our framework

Building Sustainably

Our strategy brings together ambitions, targets, activities and metrics across our most important issues to create future homes, great places and better living — protecting and enhancing the things that matter most to us: our people, the places we create, and the natural world in which we operate.

Nature

Preserving and enhancing the natural world by using resources responsibly, building resilient, low-carbon homes, and creating places where people and nature can thrive.

Natural resources

Places

Designing and building great places that meet the highest standards and promote sustainable, healthy and happy living for our customers.

Green finance

People

Everyone has the right to be respected and treated fairly at work. We nurture diverse talent and prioritise the health, safety and wellbeing of our people and partners.

Safety and wellbeing

Climate

Building a net zero future

Our net zero roadmap sets out our climate ambition, targets and implementation strategy — from the embodied carbon in our homes to the energy they use and the nature they sit within.

We are working across our operations and supply chain to cut emissions, enable the transition for our customers and build resilience into every community.

Net zero operations Low-carbon homes Climate resilience Nature recovery

Governance and data

Accountable at every level

Sustainability is integrated into how we work at every level — from site planning and material choices to water, waste and energy management — with oversight from our leadership team.
Sustainability-related risks and opportunities are assessed, managed and mitigated as part of our integrated risk management approach, from climate resilience to biodiversity and social value.
We track our environmental and social performance each year — from emissions and waste to safety and community investment — and share the results openly.
We listen carefully to our customers, colleagues, communities, partners and local authorities — and their views shape our priorities and the places we create.

Our contribution

Value beyond the homes we build

Each year we quantify the socio-economic contribution we make to the communities in which we operate — through new homes of all types and tenures.
