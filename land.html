Land

Working with us

We want to hear from you

We are one of the region's leading homebuilders and we are always looking for land and partnership opportunities in the right locations — from small infill sites to large strategic projects.

Our experienced land and planning teams work flexibly with landowners, agents, local authorities and institutional partners to bring great places forward.

Submit a site

What we look for

Sites of every shape and size

Strategic land

Large-scale, long-term opportunities with the potential for garden communities.

Urban regeneration

Complex brownfield and mixed-use sites in well-connected locations.

Immediate land

Deliverable sites with or without planning permission, ready to progress.

Partnerships

Joint ventures, public sector partnerships and package deals.

Our process

From first conversation to completed place

Step 1

Introduce your site

Share the details with our land team — location, size, ownership and planning status.

Step 2

Assessment

We review the opportunity quickly and professionally, and come back to you with our view.

Step 3

Agreement

Flexible deal structures — unconditional, conditional or option — built around your needs.

Step 4

Delivery

Planning, design and construction delivered by teams with decades of local experience.
