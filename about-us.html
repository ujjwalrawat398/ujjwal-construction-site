Building sustainably

Sustainability at the heart of every decision

We are determined to be the leading national sustainable homebuilder. Our Building Sustainably Framework brings together our ambitions, targets, activities and metrics to ensure sustainability is deeply rooted in every business decision and every day-to-day action that we take.

How we build sustainably

Placemaking

Creating great places to live

We know our customers want more than the perfect home — they want to live in a great place with a warm and welcoming community. We take a thoughtful approach to placemaking, designing places that offer opportunities to live happily, healthily and sustainably, with space for communities to grow over time.

That includes accessible, inclusive and imaginative play space in every community we create, including a new inclusive play guide developed with inclusion and accessibility experts for all new developments in planning from 2026.
