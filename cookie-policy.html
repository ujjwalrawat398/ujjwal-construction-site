Cookie policy

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how pages are used.

How we use cookies

We use essential cookies to make the site work (for example, remembering your cookie choice) and optional analytics cookies to understand which pages are popular so we can improve the experience.

Managing cookies

You can accept or decline optional cookies using the banner shown on your first visit, or clear cookies at any time in your browser settings. Declining optional cookies will not stop the website from working.
